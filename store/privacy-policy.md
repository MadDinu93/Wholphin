# Thorphin privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Thorphin is a client for Jellyfin media servers. It has no servers of its own, and its developer does not collect, receive or share any data about you.

## What the app sends, and where

Thorphin connects only to servers you choose to sign in to:

- **Your Jellyfin server**, at the address you enter. The app sends it your sign-in details and uses it to browse and play your media, and to save your watch progress and settings there.
- **Your Seerr server**, if you set one up, to browse and request titles.
- **Addresses your server gives the app**, such as artwork and trailers, which the app loads from where your server says they are.

When the app crashes, it asks whether to send a crash report. Only if you agree is the report, which contains technical details about the app and your device, sent to your own Jellyfin server. App logs are sent the same way, only when you choose to send them from the settings.

## What stays on your device

Your server addresses, user names, access tokens and app settings are stored on your device so you stay signed in. They are removed when you sign out, remove a server or uninstall the app.

## Voice search

When you search by voice, Android's speech recognition service turns your speech into text and gives the app only the text. That service is part of your device, under its own privacy terms.

## Children

Thorphin is not directed at children and shows only content from your own server.
